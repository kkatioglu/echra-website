Child Safety Standards

Effective: 24 October 2026

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

ECHRA strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualization of minors, sextortion involving minors, trafficking, or any attempt to solicit, share, create, distribute, promote, or facilitate such content or conduct.

Adults only

ECHRA is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. Users who are known or reasonably suspected to be under 18 may be restricted or removed from the service.

Reporting and blocking

Users can report and block accounts or content through ECHRA's in-app reporting and safety controls. Reports involving suspected CSAE or CSAM are prioritized for review and appropriate action.

Enforcement

ECHRA may remove content, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant records where legally permitted, and take other protective measures when these standards are violated. We do not tolerate attempts to evade enforcement or repeatedly recreate abusive accounts.

Reporting to authorities

ECHRA complies with applicable child safety laws and cooperates with lawful requests from competent authorities. Where required by applicable law, suspected CSAM or related child-safety offenses may be reported to the appropriate authorities or designated reporting organizations.

Child safety contact

Questions, urgent safety concerns, or reports related to child safety can be sent to kkdigitalprojects+echra@gmail.com. Please do not email illegal imagery or files. Provide only the information needed to identify the account, message, or incident.

Related policies

These standards supplement ECHRA's Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Support information.
